Air India - Domestic Flights Baggage Rules

1. Checked baggage allowance (domestic)
Economy class passengers may check one bag up to 15 kg.
Business class passengers may check bags up to 25 kg in total.
Bags above the allowance are charged an excess baggage fee per kilogram.

2. Cabin baggage (domestic)
Economy class passengers may carry one cabin bag up to 7 kg.
Business class passengers may carry one cabin bag up to 10 kg.
Each passenger may also carry one personal item such as a handbag
or laptop bag.

3. Power banks (domestic)
Power banks must be carried in cabin baggage only and are not allowed in
checked baggage. Power banks up to 100 Wh are allowed. Power banks above
100 Wh need airline approval before travel.

4. Prohibited items (domestic)
Knives, sharp tools and sports bats are not allowed in cabin baggage.
Lighters and matches must be carried on the person.
Electronic devices
Laptops, tablets and cameras are allowed in cabin baggage. Laptops must be
taken out of the bag during security screening.

Toiletries
Shampoo, perfume, creams and other toiletries count as liquids. In cabin
baggage they must follow the liquids rule: containers of 100 ml or less, all
placed in one transparent resealable bag of up to 1 litre.
