Air India - International Flights Baggage Rules

1. Checked baggage allowance (international)
Economy class passengers may check two bags of up to 23 kg each.
Business class passengers may check two bags of up to 32 kg each.
Bags above the allowance are charged an excess baggage fee per kilogram.

2. Cabin baggage (international)
Economy class passengers may carry one cabin bag up to 8 kg.
Business class passengers may carry two cabin bags up to 7 kg each.
Each passenger may also carry one personal item such as a handbag
or laptop bag.

3. Power banks (international)
Power banks must be carried in cabin baggage only and are not allowed in
checked baggage. Power banks up to 100 Wh are allowed without approval.
Power banks between 100 Wh and 160 Wh need airline approval, with a maximum
of two per passenger. Power banks above 160 Wh are not allowed.

4. Liquids (international)
Liquids in cabin baggage must be in containers of 100 ml or less, all placed
in one transparent resealable bag of up to 1 litre.

5. Prohibited items (international)
Knives, scissors with blades over 6 cm and sports bats are not allowed in
cabin baggage. Aerosols over 500 ml are not allowed in checked baggage.
Electronic devices
Laptops, tablets and cameras are allowed in cabin baggage. Laptops must be
taken out of the bag during security screening.

Toiletries
Shampoo, perfume, creams and other toiletries count as liquids. In cabin
baggage they must follow the liquids rule: containers of 100 ml or less, all
placed in one transparent resealable bag of up to 1 litre.
